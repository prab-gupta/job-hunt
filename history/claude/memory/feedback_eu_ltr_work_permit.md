EU LTR (Italy) allows residence in other EU states but employer must file a work permit (TWV in NL) for the first 12 months. This creates legal liability the employer takes on.

**Why:** TOPdesk HR rejected candidacy Aug 2026 despite salary fit (€42-63k range) purely because of work permit liability. Their words: "company does not want to take liability" and "range not enough to sponsor."

**How to apply:**
- Never claim "no sponsorship needed in EU" on non-Italy roles. Say "no sponsorship needed" ONLY for Italy-based or remote-from-Italy roles.
- For NL/DE/other EU onsite: say "EU Long-Term Residence Permit, employer involvement needed for work permit but lighter than full sponsorship"
- Best targets: remote-Italy, EOR companies (Deel/Remote/Oyster), companies with existing sponsor license (erkend referent in NL), high-budget roles where permit cost is rounding error (€65k+)
- Before investing time in non-Italy EU onsite roles, check if company explicitly hires non-passport EU residents
- Salary calibration: NL mid-level dev €55-70k base + 8% holiday; user's 45-55k ask flagged as "too low" for NL market
