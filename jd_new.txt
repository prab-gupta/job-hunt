### Marta Della Valle

#### Co-Founder & CEO @Mastro HR

[](https://www.linkedin.com/login?emailAddress=&fromSignIn=&session_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fit.linkedin.com%2Fin%2Fmarta-della-valle&trk=public_jobs_message-the-recruiter-cta)

_[Based in Milan or Barcelona Office]_

Pillar was founded in 2025. Today, we are 80+ people, serve 900+ customers, raised €12 million in 2026, and are expanding internationally.

We are building the financial operating system for construction: bringing AI, automation, and modern software to an enormous industry still full of slow, fragmented processes.

**We are looking for an AI Engineer who knows how to turn LLMs into reliable product experiences, moves fast, and wants to build.**

**The mission**

Build production-grade AI systems that solve real customer problems.

We want someone who can move beyond prototypes and build agents, retrieval systems, document pipelines, and AI-native product features that are reliable, measurable, and deeply integrated into Pillar.

**What you will do**

*   Own AI features end to end, from product problem to production.
*   Design and build agentic workflows using tool orchestration, structured outputs, and multi-step reasoning.
*   Build RAG and semantic search pipelines using embeddings, vector databases, hybrid search, and re-ranking.
*   Develop document extraction workflows that turn invoices, reports, and forms into structured data.
*   Build observability and evaluation systems for AI features, including tracing, experimentation, and automated quality checks.
*   Optimize context, retrieval, and prompting strategies to improve accuracy and product quality.
*   Work across our TypeScript stack when AI features require it, from backend logic to product integration.
*   Use AI as a native part of your own engineering workflow, not only as something you build for customers.
*   Move quickly from ambiguity to working product, making pragmatic technical decisions along the way.

**Our stack**

*   **Product:** Next.js · React · TypeScript
*   **Backend & data:** Supabase · Node/Deno · Postgres
*   **AI:** Mastra · LLM APIs · embeddings · vector search · AI-native workflows

**Who we are looking for**

**AI engineers with product mindset and high agency**

*   You have experience building LLM-based applications in production, not only prototypes.
*   You are comfortable with agents, tool use, structured outputs, and orchestration.
*   You have hands-on experience with RAG, embeddings, vector databases, and retrieval strategies.
*   You are comfortable with TypeScript or can become productive in it quickly.
*   You have worked with AI orchestration frameworks such as Mastra, LangChain, CrewAI, or similar.
*   You are strong with SQL and relational databases.
*   You understand prompt design, evaluation, and the trade-offs involved in building reliable AI systems.
*   You have worked in a startup or scaleup environment and understand the pace, ambiguity, and ownership it requires.
*   You take initiative, get your hands dirty, and get things done without waiting for perfect instructions.
*   You can zoom out to understand the full product problem and zoom in when technical details matter.
*   You communicate openly, learn quickly, and collaborate without ego.

**How we work**

*   Agile and squad-based: small teams own their domains, with no unnecessary layers.
*   AI-first: we build and work in the age of AI.
*   Product ownership: engineers are hands-on and accountable for delivered value.
*   Bottom-up: initiative is expected, not delegated.

**The profile that can succeed**

**AI-native · Product-minded · Startup DNA**

The sweet spot: a hands-on engineer who has already built real AI products, ideally in a startup or scaleup environment, and knows how to combine LLM capabilities, software engineering, data, and product judgment to ship reliable features.

**Location & languages**

Milan or Barcelona; professional working English.

**Hiring process**

**01:** CV screening.

**02:** 30-min Talent Call - motivation, Pillar knowledge, mindset, and startup fit.

**03:** Two 30-min Technical Interviews - one focused on AI systems and architecture, one focused on product engineering and execution.

**04:** Final Interview with our CTO.

**What we offer**

*   Gross salary **55,000-75,000 €** depending on experience.
*   The opportunity to **build core parts of Pillar’s AI product and engineering foundations** as we scale.
*   **End-to-end ownership**: from AI architecture and experimentation to shipping features used by real customers.
*   The chance to work in an **AI-native engineering team**, where AI is part of both the product and the way we build it.
*   Close collaboration with **Product, AI, and Engineering**, with direct exposure to the problems we are solving for customers.
*   **Real autonomy, responsibility, and room to grow** as Pillar and the engineering team scale.
