To Whom It May Concern,

I am writing to recommend Prabhavit Gupta, who worked with me at FlowSpace from March 2024 to August 2026. I am the founder and CEO of FlowSpace, a coworking marketplace platform serving over 4,500 users and hundreds of workspace providers across the UK.

Prabhavit joined as our primary engineer and quickly became the technical backbone of the company. He was responsible for the full stack, but what set him apart was his ability to take a business problem, figure out the right technical solution, and ship it without hand-holding.

Three things stand out from his time with us:

First, he built our AI-powered listing pipeline from scratch. We needed a way to take workspace provider information in any format - PDFs, spreadsheets, photos, web pages - and turn it into structured, publish-ready listings. Prabhavit designed and built the entire system: document ingestion, AI extraction using Claude and OpenAI APIs, human review workflow, and automated push to our platform. It processed over 900 listings and became a core part of how we onboard new inventory.

Second, he owned our CRM integrations end to end. He integrated OfficeRnD and Nexudus - the two main software platforms our workspace providers use - building the OAuth connection flows, location syncing, resource mapping, and automated booking pipelines. These were not simple API calls. Each integration required understanding the provider's business model, mapping their data to ours, and handling edge cases in production. He was the direct technical contact for providers during onboarding, configuring their accounts and resolving issues himself.

Third, he led the migration of our entire platform off a legacy no-code stack to Next.js and Laravel. He mapped the data model, planned the cutover, and executed it while the product stayed live for thousands of active users. That kind of work requires careful planning and a strong sense of what can go wrong.

Beyond these projects, Prabhavit built workflow automations using n8n that handled lead routing, data synchronisation, and support queries. He did not wait for me to identify what needed automating. He would notice a repetitive process, propose a solution, and build it. Several of our most useful internal automations came from him spotting an inefficiency before anyone else flagged it. He built AI-powered support agents that could answer user questions against our live platform data. He set up dashboards and internal tools that helped the team operate more efficiently.

He also dealt directly with our customers on a regular basis. When workspace providers had technical issues, configuration questions, or needed help understanding how to use the platform, Prabhavit was their first point of contact. He handled these interactions with patience and clarity, adapting his explanations to each provider's technical level. He was just as comfortable walking a non-technical office manager through a setup as he was debugging an API issue with a provider's own development team. That customer-facing flexibility is rare in someone who is also a strong builder.

What I valued most about working with Prabhavit was his combination of technical ability and communication skills. He could sit in a call with a non-technical workspace provider, understand their operational needs, and translate that into a working solution. He was equally comfortable writing code, debugging a production issue at short notice, or explaining a technical decision to me in plain terms. He also coordinated effectively with our external development team, keeping them aligned on priorities, flagging blockers early, and making sure nothing fell through the cracks between what he was building and what they were delivering. He maintained clear, consistent communication across time zones and never needed to be chased for updates.

Prabhavit is not someone who is locked into one language or framework. At FlowSpace he worked across TypeScript, PHP, and Python depending on what the job needed. Our AI listing pipeline and data processing scripts were built in Python, while the main platform runs on Next.js and Laravel. He picked up whatever was required and delivered production-quality work in each. That flexibility mattered to us because as a small team, we could not afford to have someone who only works in one stack.

He is self-directing, reliable, and genuinely cares about the quality of what he ships. I would not hesitate to recommend him for any role involving AI implementation, systems integration, automation, or client-facing technical work.

Please feel free to contact me if you would like to discuss Prabhavit's work in more detail.
