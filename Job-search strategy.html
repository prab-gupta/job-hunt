4The follow-up cadence — your #1 fix

This single habit recovers the losses your funnel stats blamed on follow-up gaps. For every pitch, application, and cold message:
  • Day 0 — send (hook + proof + soft ask).
  • Day 3–4 — short bump ("floating this back up — happy to send a 2-min demo").
  • Day 8–10 — final value-add ("built a quick mock of what I'd do for you — worth a look?") then mark closed.
No exceptions. Two-thirds of replies come from the follow-ups, not the first message.

7Pipeline hygiene — two rules

One tracker is the source of truth. Log every touch with a next action + date. If it's not in the tracker with a next step, it doesn't exist.
Never cold-touch a company twice. Some names appear in more than one sheet (e.g. Qdrant is in both your outreach and freelance sheets). Pick one channel per company. You've applied to 637 already — check before you send.

8Say yes / say no

Say YES to anything that starts a conversation with a named human, a paid trial, a productized-offer pitch, or a high-fit contract. Speed of reply matters more than polish.
Say NO to re-applying cold to saturated boards, sub-€150 gigs (unless pure portfolio), equity-only (unless you want the bet), and anything needing German/French fluency or a CS degree.

Productize the listing bot and pitch the cluster this week. It's your differentiator, it's low-competition, it's the fastest path to cash, and every pitch doubles as interview proof for the contract/FT roles. Everything else in this plan supports that one move.
