## Instruction and control failure

The session had read a rule stating: "Answer every question from his CV, own letter, or stated facts. Never invent portfolio, referral, demographic answer, relocation commitment, biographical detail." This appears at main log line 327, dated September 18.

The user's instruction at main line 1247 required custom CVs for applications outside Easy Apply. The later instruction at line 3740 said to continue applying to FDE roles. These instructions authorized tailoring and applications; they did not authorize fabricating credentials.

Observable failure: the agent replaced established candidate facts during generation, then passed the result into an autonomous application workflow without a recorded factual comparison or user review. Compilation and punctuation checks could not detect false content. The logs do not establish why the model ignored the facts or rules.

The false degree also appears in Cohere, Nostrion, and Devoteam CVs written in this same batch. This audit establishes their generation; it does not comprehensively audit all applications from that session.
