## What the sweep taught (recorded in market-intelligence.md and Search notes)

Deel's Ashby API returns an empty array while they are clearly hiring - a misleading negative. Automation tool vendors are EMPTY in EMEA this month: his Tray.ai and Microsoft certs cash in at partner consultancies, not at the certifying vendors. HR-tech is thin but non-zero. Track D at his level with EU-only rights is genuinely scarce: two live rows total.
