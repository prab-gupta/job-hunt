## A feature change or improvement that impacted user growth

> At FlowSpace the platform went from 2,000 to over 4,500 active users while I owned the
> automation side. The change with the clearest effect was the scraping and parsing pipeline that
> increased the number of listings: supply was the constraint on activation, because a member who
> searched and found nothing near them didn't come back. Automating listing ingestion rather than
> chasing it manually moved that. The second was consolidating lead capture into the CRM with
> enrichment, which cut the time between someone showing interest and someone following up —
> before that, leads sat in three places and some were simply never seen.

## What aspects of growth engineering work are most meaningful to you?

> The part I like is that growth work is measurable in a way most engineering isn't: you ship a
> change and the funnel either moves or it doesn't, and you can't argue with it. It also sits
> where I'm most useful — between the product and the systems around it, wiring CRM, analytics and
> automation together so the business can see what's happening. And it rewards shipping small
> things quickly, which suits how I work with AI tooling: build the rough version, put it in front
> of real usage, keep what survives.
