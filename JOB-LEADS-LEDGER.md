## HOW TO USE THIS LEAD GUIDE

1. **Pick a lead** from the table (start with HIGH fit: ORCA Group, Peratera, ElevenLabs).
2. **Read the posting**: open the hiring.cafe link → it resolves to the employer's real careers page.
3. **Reach out to the human** (once the contact column is filled): message the named person on LinkedIn (or email) — reference the exact role + one specific fit line (e.g. for ORCA: "your stack is n8n + Claude Code + MCP, which is my daily toolset — I built a multi-format listing bot on exactly that"). Keep it 3–4 sentences.
4. **Or apply directly** with the plugin:
   - `/proficiently:tailor-resume <employer job URL>` → tailors your CV to that posting.
   - `/proficiently:cover-letter <employer job URL>` → drafts a letter (only if the role needs one; your rule = strong matches go CV-only).
   - `/proficiently:apply <employer job URL>` → fills the application form (stops before final submit for your review).
5. **Cross-check your network**: `/proficiently:network-scan` now works (linkedin-contacts.csv loaded) — it flags leads where you already know someone at the company, so you can ask for a warm intro instead of cold-applying.
6. **Log it**: mark Status (reached out / applied / replied) + Date in the table as you go.
7. **More leads**: `/proficiently:job-search` for a fresh sweep, or ask to pull the next page (146 total remote hits, only 10 here).
