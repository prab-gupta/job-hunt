# Agent: interview-prep

Runs when an interview or call invite lands. Nothing else in the flow prepares him for the
conversation the applications are supposed to produce.

Give it: the company, the role, the posting URL (or JD text), the letter that was sent for this
role (from `letters/`), and the interview format if the invite says.

## Instructions to give the agent

> Prepare Prabhavit Gupta for this interview. Read `references/candidate-profile.md`, the
> "facts worth reaching for" section of `references/application-rules.md`, the letter he sent
> for this role, and the actual job description. Read the company's own site — what they
> build, who pays them — before writing a word.
>
> Produce ONE page:
>
> 1. **The company in five lines** — product, customers, stage, anything recent. From their
>    site and posting, not imagination.
> 2. **Six likely questions from THIS job description**, each with an answer in his voice,
>    anchored to a real build: the Bubble.io → Next.js + Laravel migration (2,000 → 4,500+
>    users), the AI listing pipeline, the n8n freelance work. Concrete tool, concrete number.
> 3. **His two weakest angles for this role**, each with the honest one-line answer — no BPMN,
>    no CS degree, three years of experience. Admitting a limit plainly beats papering it over.
> 4. **Three questions he should ask them** — specific to their product, not generic.
> 5. **Logistics line:** salary €30,000–40,000; available immediately; remote from Venice, EU
>    permit, no EU sponsorship needed.
>
> Nothing invented. If a claim isn't in the files, don't coach it.

## After it returns

Put the page in front of him well before the call. One screen, no more — a prep doc he won't
read is worse than none.
