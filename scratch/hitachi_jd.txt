Job description

• 15+ years of experience in designing and implementing enterprise integrations for PTC Windchill PLM.
• Design, develop, and support integrations between Windchill and enterprise systems such as ERP, ALM, SLM, MES, SharePoint, and custom applications.
• Develop and consume REST APIs, web services, middleware interfaces, and messaging frameworks for seamless data exchange.
• Prepare interface specifications, data mappings, and integration design documents based on business requirements.
• Collaborate with solution architects, application teams, and third-party vendors to deliver scalable and secure integration solutions.
• Troubleshoot integration issues, optimize interface performance, and support deployment, testing, and production releases.
• Support effort estimation, technical proposals, and integration strategy for PLM implementation programs.
• Ensure integration best practices, security standards, and governance across enterprise PLM ecosystems.)
