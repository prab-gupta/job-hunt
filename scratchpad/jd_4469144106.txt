 About the job 
 Growy · Full-time · Based in Milan · Travel between Italy and Malta 
 Growy exists to solve enterprise AI's real bottleneck: context. In every company, people and systems fail not for lack of intelligence but because the right context isn't available at the right moment — knowledge is dispersed across tools, inboxes, and heads. Growy is the context layer for both humans and agents: a living 
 Company Brain 
 that unifies dispersed knowledge, keeps itself current with every action, and lets people and agents act on it autonomously. We map the company into the Brain, agents run on top of it, and every change in the company updates the context — so agents never work from stale knowledge. Intuitive and built for non-technical users, Growy lets any employee achieve complex, multi-step operational tasks. 
 You'll work on live client deployments from day one, travelling between Italy and Malta to be where our clients are. 
 The role 
 You support clients through the initial phase — setting up their Company Brain, mapping their processes and structures, and implementing their first workflows. Then you teach them to do it themselves, so their teams build and extend workflows autonomously. From there, you're their point of contact: the person they trust when they want to go further. 
 You're the first of many. As we grow, you'll hire and train the next FDEs — duplicating yourself and building the deployment team you'll lead. 
 What you'll do 
 Map the client's processes, structures, and systems — including the genuinely complex ones — into the Company Brain, and set up how that context stays current as the company changes 
 Design, implement, and debug the first agentic workflows end-to-end: prompt design, tool configuration, error handling, human-in-the-loop approvals 
 Connect the client's stack through Growy's 3,000+ built-in integrations (Microsoft 365, ERPs, CRMs, and more) — the work is making sure the client's setup is right, not building connectors from scratch 
 Train the client's internal champions — non-technical users included — to build workflows on their own 
 Be the client's ongoing point of contact as they expand, travelling to client sites in Italy and Malta 
 Hire and onboard the next FDEs as the client base grows — turning how you work into how the team works 
 Who you are 
 Half engineer, half consultant. The ideal profile has a tech and AI background and has worked client-side — you can map a business process in a workshop in the morning and debug the workflow that automates it in the afternoon. 
 Fast learner, relentless executor: you pick up new tools, systems, and domains in days, not months — and you ship. No hand-holding, no waiting for perfect instructions; you figure it out and get it done 
 Technical: a solid tech and AI foundation — you're hands-on with LLMs, agents, and integrations, and nothing about APIs, logs, or debugging scares you 
 Consultant: process mapping, stakeholder management, turning business cases into buildable workflows 
 A natural teacher: you make people capable, not dependent 
 Fluent English required; fluent Italian strongly preferred 
 Willing to travel regularly between Italy and Malta 
 What you get 
 Live client deployments from day one, direct exposure to the founders 
 Unlimited room to grow — the clear path is from first FDE to leading the deployment team as we expand across Europe 
 Competitive salary and performance bonus 
 Travel expenses fully covered, flexible working, and the latest AI tools to work with
