 About ReBloom ReBloom is an AI-native fintech platform headquartered in Copenhagen, building the leading technology infrastructure for private market secondary transactions bringing public market sophistication like Dark Pools, IOI matching, and AI-powered Order Management Systems to private secondaries. Our institutional clients include some of the most prominent names in pre-IPO tech. We are profitable, our pipeline is compounding, and we are now building the team to match our momentum. The Opportunity We're looking for a Product Engineer to help build ReBloom's platform from the inside out. In this role you'll be in the room as we figure out what to build next, translating a fast-moving product vision into shipped software, and shaping that vision in return. You'll work fullstack across our platform and our AI infrastructure, with direct exposure to the founding team and real influence over direction. This is a role for someone who wants to build a company, not just write code for one. What You'll Do Ship fullstack features across ReBloom's core platform from database and API work to the interfaces our institutional clients use daily Work directly with the founding team to turn product ideas into working software, iterating quickly as the vision evolves Build and extend our AI tooling of agents, retrieval, and workflows using our stack (Langchain, MCP, and related tools) that power features like the AI enabled Order Management System. Own infrastructure decisions across AWS and Vercel, balancing speed with reliability as we scale Bring product judgment, not just implementation. Have a mentality of iterating fast and adjust when something doesn't serve the client or the business Move fluidly between building, debugging, and thinking about what we should build next What We're Looking For 3–5 years of fullstack engineering experience, comfortable owning a feature end-to-end. Someone that has been hands on in building a product early Working knowledge of AWS, Vercel, and modern AI application tooling (Langchain, MCP, or equivalent).
