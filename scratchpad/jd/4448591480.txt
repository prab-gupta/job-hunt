Job Title: Full Stack Developer - CT Office Incubation Team Location: London, UK; Cambridge, UK; or Milan, Italy – (Hybrid, 50% On-site) Employment Type: Full-time The job AVEVA’s Chief Technology Office drives emerging-technology innovation from early signals to real-world impact by scouting technology and market trends and then maturing capabilities such as Industrial AI, Digital Twins, robotics, simulation, visualization, and edge-to-cloud technologies through hands-on technical evaluation, experimentation, and rapid prototyping. The CT Office Incubation Team turns emerging technology ideas into working prototypes that can be validated with customers, partners, and internal teams. As a Full Stack Developer, you will help build end-to-end prototype solutions (UI, APIs, data integration, and cloud/edge deployment) to test new concepts in Digital Twins, Industrial AI, robotics, simulation, and visualization. You’ll work in small, fast-moving, multidisciplinary teams, collaborating with engineers, researchers, product and solution specialists, and industrial domain experts. This role suits an early-career developer who enjoys learning quickly, working across the stack, and turning ideas into functional software—valuing curiosity, practical problem-solving, and clear communication alongside coding skills. Key Responsibilities Support the build of full‑stack prototypes by implementing and testing frontend, backend, APIs, and basic data integrations under guidance. Help turn product ideas into small, testable demos by contributing to minimum-viable implementations and iterating quickly. Develop and maintain simple, well-documented REST APIs and integrations (including event-driven patterns when needed) for internal and customer-facing prototype environments. Use AI-assisted coding tools effectively and responsibly, while learning to validate generated code for correctness, security, quality, and IP compliance with support from senior engineers. Improve reliability and demo readiness by fixing bugs, adding basic tests, and ensuring prototypes can be set up and reproduced by others. Apply baseline security best practices (secrets handling, dependency hygiene, access control awareness, safe data handling) following team standards. Troubleshoot prototype applications using logs and basic observability tools; escalate issues early with clear context. Document your work clearly (setup steps, API notes, known limitations, assumptions) to help teammates and future reuse. Collaborate with cross-functional teammates (product, researchers, solution architects, domain experts) and participate in team practices such as PR reviews, while building confidence in providing feedback. Contribute small reusable components and templates and grow your knowledge of industrial software topics (Digital Twins, simulation, industrial data, robotics, edge/visualization) over time. Essential Requirements Bachelor’s or Master’s degree (or equivalent hands-on experience) in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Engineering, Maths/Physics, or a related field. 1–3 years of practical coding experience through a mix of internships, junior roles, university projects, open-source, or personal projects. Experience building end-to-end applications or prototypes, covering both backend and basic frontend components. Working knowledge of at least one backend stack (e.g., Python, Rust, Java, Node.js, C) and a willingness to learn new tools as needed. Familiarity with REST APIs, basic database concepts (relational and/or NoSQL), and software engineering fundamentals (testing, debugging, modular design, incremental delivery). Comfortable using Git and common team workflows (branches, pull requests, code reviews, CI/CD). Experience using AI-assisted development tools (e.g., Copilot/Cursor/Claude Code/Codex), with an understanding that generated code must be reviewed and validated. Exposure to containers (Docker/Podman/Apptainer) and at least one cloud platform (Azure/AWS/GCP). Initial experience with edge and autonomous deployments, for example deploying software to constrained devices or platforms such as robots, drones, gateways, or on-prem edge servers, including awareness of reliability and connectivity Able to work in ambiguous problem spaces, ask good questions, learn quickly, and communicate clearly in English; willing to travel occasionally for workshops, demos, and prototype validation.
