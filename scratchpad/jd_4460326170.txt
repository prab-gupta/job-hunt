 About the job 
 At IMS Nanofabrication, we shape the future — one nanometer at a time. Headquartered in Austria, with subsidiaries in Vienna, Taiwan, South Korea, and the USA, we are a 
 global leader in semiconductor technology 
 . Our cutting-edge 
 mask writers power the world's most advanced chip manufacturing 
 , making high-performance smartphones, PCs, and data centers possible. By pushing the boundaries of nanofabrication, we enable innovation at the smallest scale — driving progress on a global level. 
 Job Description 
 Are you passionate about cutting-edge semiconductor technology and want to work in an environment where software meets hardware at the highest level? As part of our software support team at IMS, you will be responsible for deploying and integrating our multi-beam mask writer software in high-tech fabrication plants around the world — directly at the sites of the world's leading chip manufacturers. You will continuously support the automation and optimization of these tasks, drive project ownership, and contribute to improving our tools and processes. If you thrive in hands-on, international environments and enjoy solving complex technical challenges, we want to hear from you. 
 Your Responsibilities 
 Deploy and integrate our multi-beam mask writer software at IMS and on customer sites 
 Prepare tool-specific configurations and perform regular software and configuration upgrades on customer and IMS tools and testbenches 
 Conduct FAT (factory acceptance tests) including full documentation 
 Troubleshoot issues by analyzing data, identifying root causes, and proposing solutions 
 Report troubleshooting activities, observations, test outcomes, and proposed solutions via Jira ticketing system 
 Provide expert support to onsite engineers 
 Drive ongoing improvement, automation, and optimization of team tasks 
 Collaborate closely with R&D engineers and other stakeholders to develop best solutions 
 Create technical documentation 
 Take ownership of projects and work independently 
 Fulfill on-call duties (one weekend every two months) 
 Qualifications 
 A technical degree (e.g., computer science, electrical engineering, physics) or at least 3 years of relevant technical experience 
 Very good knowledge of Linux-based operating systems (understanding of system architectures) 
 Solid understanding of networking fundamentals 
 Analytical thinking and the ability to tackle complex challenges 
 Resilience and the ability to perform well under pressure 
 Flexibility and willingness to travel a few times per year (up to one month per trip) 
 A hands-on mentality and customer-oriented thinking 
 Strong communication skills and a collaborative mindset 
 A proactive approach and the ability to work independently 
 Very good spoken and written English 
 Willingness to work in a clean room environment 
 Git experience is of advantage 
 Basic knowledge of Python or other programming languages would be beneficial 
 Basic data base skills are also a plus 
 Additional Information 
 What you can look forward to: 
 Travel: The chance to travel to international branches and customer sites 
 Leading Edge & High-Tech: The privilege of working in high-tech fabs at the world's leading chip manufacturers. Gain deep knowledge of our multi-beam mask writer hardware and software architecture through deployments, integration, and troubleshooting. 
 Career & Development: Exciting opportunities to grow professionally and personally - in an international environment 
 Team Culture: Dynamic, collegial and open to new ideas - together we are writing the future 
 Flexibility: Flexible working hours, the option to work from home and workation 
 Catering: Subsidized canteen, fresh fruit and drinks right in the office 
 Mobility: Shuttle bus for a comfortable journey, free parking spaces, e-charging stations for cars and motorcycles, bicycle parking spaces with e-bike charging station 
 Health & well-being: Company doctor and psychologist - support for your health 
 Bonus program: All employees benefit from our success - together we achieve more 
 Job bike: Environmentally friendly commuting and staying fit - with us you can lease your bike at a reasonable price 
 Events & team building: Summer party, business run, cool team events - we celebrate our successes together 
 Employee discounts: From car washes to wellness days - benefit from many discounts 
 With us you will receive an attractive annual gross salary from 
 50.000,- € 
 (plus bonus, KV Metallgewerbe). Depending on your experience, this can also be higher. 
 No matter where you are from, how you live, what you believe in - we look forward to receiving your application! What counts for us is the person, not the label. That's why we welcome your unique perspective. 
 We are committed to equal opportunities and want all applications to be assessed with an open mind. You are welcome to remove your personal information such as age, origin etc. from your CV. We don't need a photo either. This way we can concentrate on the essentials: your qualifications and experience!

 Competitors 
 IMS Nanofabrication is a leading player in the multi-beam mask writer market, which is a critical segment within the semiconductor lithography ecosystem. The company specializes in the production of advanced photomasks used in EUV lithography, a technology essential for next-generation semiconductor manufacturing. IMS Nanofabrication faces competition from companies like NuFlare Technology, Dai Nippon Printing Co., Ltd., and Vistec Semiconductor Systems. These competitors are also heavily invested in R&D and focus on innovations in electron beam lithography to capture market share. The competitive landscape is characterized by rapid technological advancements and strategic investments aimed at enhancing production capabilities and meeting the growing demand for high-precision photomasks.

 IMS Nanofabrication GmbH is an Austrian business and the global technology leader for multi-beam mask writers. Our customers are the largest chip manufacturers in the world, who rely on IMS' technology to produce current and future chip generations. 
 Our innovative multi-beam writers play a key role in chip manufacturing and provide significant added value to the semiconductor industry. They are continually customized and refined by an interdisciplinary team, in line with the latest market demands. 
 Over the last ten years, we have perfected our electron-based multi-beam technology. The first generation of systems (MBMW-101 – 1st generation Multi-Beam Mask Writer) is successfully operating all over the world. The 2nd generation Multi-Beam Mask Writer, MBMW-201 has entered the mask writer market in Q1 2019 for the 5nm technology node. 
 At IMS, our know-how is shaped by a diverse team of experts from a multitude of disciplines, such as physics, electronics, development, project management, supply chain management, production, service and administration. 
 “Our multi-beam technology is the key to the evolution of the semiconductor industry.” Dr. Elmar Platzgummer, CEO 
 Get started with one of Austria's most innovative companies. 
 Are you looking for new challenges in a globally acting high-tech company? Would you like to work in a multi-disciplinary and international team? 
 As the “hidden champion” of the semiconductor industry, IMS specializes in innovation for the nanotechnology sector. This technology is of high strategic importance for the world’s leading chip manufacturers and a pivotal tool for the production of today’s and tomorrow’s chip generations.
