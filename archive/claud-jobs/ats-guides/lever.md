# Lever runbook

Status: baseline. No Lever-specific failure evidence is recorded yet. Update this guide only after a verified real application.

## Safe baseline

1. Verify the named employer, role, location, and live application form.
2. Create job-specific materials under a dated company folder.
3. Upload the tailored CV and attach or enter the cover letter whenever offered.
4. Complete contact details and custom questions from canonical profile facts only.
5. Use visible UI options for autocomplete, dropdown, radio, and consent controls.
6. Leave optional marketing consent unchecked.
7. Review every value and uploaded filename.
8. Hand back CAPTCHA, account creation, passwords, anti-AI questions, and unknown personal facts.
9. Submit after required action-time confirmation.
10. Capture visible success text and screenshot before updating the tracker.

## Learning checklist

After first verified Lever run, record: URL/dead-posting signal, file-upload behavior, autocomplete behavior, validation rerenders, CAPTCHA behavior, confirmation signature, and whether failed submit preserves attachments.
