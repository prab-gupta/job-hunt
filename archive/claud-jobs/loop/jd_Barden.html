ABOUT THE ROLE:

We are seeking an AI Deployment Manager (Content Development) to drive the deployment of AI-enabled solutions across Centralized Content projects. The role will focus on improving content creation, content reuse and localisation through the evaluation, design and implementation of AI-enabled tools and workflows.


Key Responsibilities:

· Manage the pilot of generative AI solutions for creating first drafts of strategic story decks, congress updates and other priority content.

· Assess suitable tools, define priority use cases and evaluate opportunities to improve the efficiency and quality of content development.

· Develop a modular content plan and reusable content architecture for content, claims and approved content blocks.

· Define tagging, maintenance and reuse principles to enable faster adaptation of approved content across markets and channels.

· Define and test an AI-enabled localisation solution for approved GSL materials following Velocity implementation.

· Assess AI-supported translation capabilities and improve the findability of approved source materials, including through ARI.

· Collaborate with the Centralized Content team and relevant stakeholders to ensure solutions are practical, scalable and aligned with governance and approval workflows.

· Document findings, recommendations and implementation requirements, and communicate progress to key stakeholders.

· Identify opportunities to improve content supply chain efficiency through automation, standardisation and responsible use of AI.


ABOUT THE PERSON:

Required Experience and Skills

· Experience in content operations, content supply chain, digital transformation or AI-enabled workflow implementation.

· Experience evaluating or implementing AI, automation or generative AI solutions.

· Understanding of content modularisation, content reuse and localisation processes.

· Strong project management, analytical and problem-solving skills.

· Ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional and international teams.

· Experience in the pharmaceutical, healthcare or life sciences sector would be advantageous.

· Knowledge of regulated content, scientific communications and approval workflows would be an advantage
