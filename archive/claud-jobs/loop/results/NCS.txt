LEARNINGS: SmartRecruiters has at least two ATS front ends - the older jQuery-style form (seen on
other applications) and this newer "oneclick-ui" React app, which is built almost entirely out of

file_upload) cannot see inside it at all, but raw coordinate click+type still works for ordinary
text inputs, comboboxes and radios - only the file input needs Playwright. Worth adding to
ats-guides/smartrecruiters.md: check the URL for "/oneclick-ui/" and treat the resume dropzone as
shadow-blocked by default.
