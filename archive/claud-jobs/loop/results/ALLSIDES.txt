Two traps worth noting for future onboard.org applications: (1) the CV upload control is a visible paperclip button that doesn't itself expose a file input to find/read_page — the actual input is a hidden `<input type="file" id="ngf-upload-cv">` (ngFileUpload) elsewhere in the DOM; search for it by id/type rather than clicking the visible button.
