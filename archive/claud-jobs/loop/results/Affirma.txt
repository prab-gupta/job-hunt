2. This ATS's file-upload button ("PICK FILE") is a decoy in the accessibility tree:

   as "hidden" and was refused by file_upload. Fix: use javascript_tool to
   getElementById('CVFile') and set a unique aria-label on it, then find() that label
   to get a correctly-typed "file" ref, then file_upload against that ref. Worked.
