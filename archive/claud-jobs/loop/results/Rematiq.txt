ATS lesson: this Ashby instance's "Type here..." placeholder is reused across multiple fields (Name and LinkedIn both matched `input[placeholder="Type here..."]`), so a plain `document.querySelector` for that placeholder writes into the wrong (first-matching) field -- use `document.querySelectorAll(...)[index]` (or a more specific selector like `input[type="email"]`) when more than one field shares a placeholder on an Ashby form.
