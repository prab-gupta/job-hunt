# Agent: follow-up

Owns everything after submit — replies, silences, orphaned letters, blocked applications.
Nothing else in the flow does, so submitted applications currently go dark.

Run at the start of any job-hunt session (or on a schedule). Give it: the tracker's Submitted
list with dates, Gmail access, and the auditor's orphan-letter list if one exists.

## Instructions to give the agent

> Check the state of Prabhavit Gupta's submitted applications. You may read email; you draft
> replies but NEVER send anything — every outgoing message is his explicit approval, one by one.
>
> 1. Search the inbox for mail from each company on the Submitted list, including their ATS
>    domains (greenhouse.io, ashbyhq.com, lever.co, workable.com, join.com).
> 2. Classify every thread found:
>    - **INTERVIEW / CALL INVITE** — surface immediately at the top of the report; draft an
>      acceptance in his voice offering concrete availability.
>    - **ACTION NEEDED** — assessment link, questionnaire, verification mail; state the
>      deadline if one is given.
>    - **REJECTION** — mark it in the tracker, one line, no drafting.
>    - **SILENCE** — no thread at all.
> 3. For SILENCE of 10+ days: draft a two-sentence chase in his voice — restate interest with
>    one concrete fact from his CV, no "just checking in", no apology.
> 4. For each orphan letter (written but never attached — e.g. Sourcegraph): draft a short
>    follow-up email attaching it.
> 5. Note the join.com block: five roles (GTM Base, dexter health, Pinnipedia, Arkipel, Jamie)
>    open up the moment he signs in to join.com in the browser — remind him if still blocked.
>
> Report as a table: company · role · status · action drafted / needs-him. Invites first.

## After it returns

Put drafts in front of him for approval and update the tracker's status column. Sending is
always his click.
