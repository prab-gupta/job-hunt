## What to change per role

Only three things, and they carry the whole letter:

1. **The opening.** Name the role, the company, and one true, specific sentence about what they
   build — the kind of detail you only get from reading their site or the JD properly. The
   Arsenalia opening above is the standard to hit. A vague opener is the fastest way to look
   automated.
2. **The role-specific paragraph.** Mirror the actual requirements in the posting against
   things he has built, using their vocabulary where it's honest to do so.
3. **The closing line.** One sentence, tuned to what the role is about.
