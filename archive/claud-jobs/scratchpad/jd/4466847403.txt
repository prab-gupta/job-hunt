ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ENGINEER You are going to join our AI team that uses Generative-AI to build conversational interfaces for MSC service exposed to our internal users and external customers. You will orchestrate RAG and Function calling to implement the next generation of shipping chatbots. You will have the opportunity to work with the latest Microsoft AI-tools to interact and integrate shipping data with Microsoft 365 ecosystem. We are looking for developers having experience on Generative-AI technologies. MSC is a “MS stack” based using C#. Still, we are also considering developers that are currently using other languages (e.g. python), but they are willing to add C# language and MS stack to their toolbox.
