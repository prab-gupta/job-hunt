 Canali has been a paradigm of Italian tailored luxury and masculine elegance for over 90 years. Position Location Sovico (MB) Job Responsability Dynamics 365 Migration Project Participation in gathering and formalizing requirements with business functions; reconstruction of as-is processes and definition of the to-be model on D365. Fit-gap analysis against D365 standard functionality and evaluation of the choice between standard solutions and customization, including the related impact on costs and maintainability. Daily liaison with the system integrator: review of functional analyses, verification of estimates, reasoned challenge of solutions inconsistent with the process. Review of Functional Design Documents and process documentation. Setup and execution of functional testing and User Acceptance Testing; support for the migration of master data and transactional data. Key user training, support during go-live, and oversight of the stabilization phase. Current Application Support (AX 2012) Second-level support for corporate, plant, and store users; taking ownership of, analyzing, and resolving reported issues. Ordinary and evolutionary maintenance: analysis of change requests, specifications for developers, release verification. Oversight of integrations with connected systems and consistency checks on data flows between applications. Management of change requests across the DEV / TEST / UAT / PROD environments. Requirements 5+ years as an ERP functional analyst/consultant, focus on Supply Chain and/or Manufacturing (consulting, system integrator, or in-house). Hands-on knowledge of D365 F&O (or AX) across PIM, Inventory, Warehouse, Procurement, Production Control, Master Planning, and Sales and Marketing modules. Ability to run fit-gap analyses and draft Functional Design Documents. Understanding of the accounting impact of logistics/production transactions (inventory valuation, cost accounting, order-receipt-invoice reconciliation). Experience on at least one ERP implementation/upgrade project, from analysis to go-live. Experience in multi-company, multi-country environments (group of ~20 companies, Italian and international). Willingness to support the AX 2012 system during its maintenance phase. Good written and spoken English. Willingness to work in a lean IT structure, in direct contact with the business, including operational user support.
