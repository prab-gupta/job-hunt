Who We're Looking For: AI Consultant to help enterprises identify, design, and launch high-value AI agents with measurable outcomes. You'll collaborate cross-functionally with AI engineers, product, solution engineering, sales, and customer success teams to ensure agents are deployed, scaled, and delivering high ROI to our customers' businesses. Key Responsibilities: Collaborate with customers to develop AI adoption roadmaps and identify potential opportunities for AI use cases. Gain a deep understanding of each customer's domain and business processes to prioritize the most impactful AI use cases, based on business impact and technical feasibility. Translate business problems and opportunities into solution concepts, requirements, and implementation proposals, with success metrics and guardrails. Design AI agent solutions and redesign workflows around them — removing repetitive work, unlocking new possibilities. Define how AI agents integrate with enterprise systems — CRM, ERP, SaaS applications, APIs, MCPs, workflows. Prepare agent specifications and solution documentation that enable delivery teams to implement successfully. Collaborate with Sales, Customer Success, Product, and Delivery teams across pre-sales, implementation, and adoption. Support customer presentations and executive discussions around AI strategy. Stay up to date with emerging AI technologies, agentic AI trends, and industry best practices. Required Skills & Competencies: Strong skills in business analysis, process design, value mapping, and discovery. Familiarity with modern LLMs, AI agent architecture, and business process automation; comfort with APIs and integrations. Experience in Solution Consulting, AI Consulting, Solution Implementation, Business Consulting, or a similar customer-facing role. Hands-on experience designing or launching AI-powered solutions or AI agents. Experience translating business requirements into solution designs. Experience working with enterprise customers and multiple stakeholders. Excellent executive communication and presentation skills. Strong analytical and systems thinking. Excellent spoken and written English.
