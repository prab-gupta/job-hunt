The Opportunity The AI enablement team, part of the Teradyne AI organization, is accelerating the deployment of AI-augmented workflows across the entire organization. As Technical Lead Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering, you lead a regional pod of Forward Deployed Engineers while remaining its primary technical expert. This is a technical leadership role first. You architect solutions, establish reusable patterns, and stay hands-on in delivery, while also managing a team of three to ten engineers. You own AI enablement outcomes for your region, working with business units that sit in your time zone and reporting into the Central AI team. Regional pods cover the Americas, Europe, and Asia, and you will partner with your peer TLMs to keep tooling, patterns and quality consistent worldwide. Responsibilities Set the technical direction for your region's AI enablement work: solution architecture, platform choices, reusable patterns, and the quality bar for what ships. Stay hands-on. Lead, coach and grow a regional team of Forward Deployed Engineers; own hiring, performance and career development for your pod. Own the relationship with business units in your region — diagnose workflow bottlenecks, scope what AI can realistically solve, and turn that into a prioritized pipeline.
