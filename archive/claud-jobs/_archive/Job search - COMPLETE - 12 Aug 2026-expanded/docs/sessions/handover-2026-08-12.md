## Method findings worth keeping

- **Aggregator mirrors are not verification.** Roles verified only via RemoteRocketship (Space Inch, Action1, Quadrivia) were all dead on the real ATS. join.com metadata lied about freshness twice (Novosafe said "updated today", live page said archived 6 months ago; SERA the same).

- Attrition tonight was roughly **50%** — 21 of the roles the sweeps returned were dead, gated or hard-blocked when opened in a browser.
