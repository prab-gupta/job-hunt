# Blindspot sweep, 12 August 2026

Prompted by the question "from my profile and CV what are my blindspots that I have not aimed for?" Eight categories were identified where his CV evidence is strong and he had never applied. Two sweeps ran before the session's web search budget was exhausted (200/200).

## The eight blindspots

1. **Automation tool vendors themselves** — he holds Tray.ai Build Practitioner + Foundations and three Microsoft AI/Power Platform certs. Thinnest applicant pool available to him.
2. **Microsoft Power Platform / Copilot Studio partner consultancies** — his certs are the actual currency; Capco surfaced by accident.
3. **Coworking / proptech** — he has integrated **both OfficeRnD and Nexudus** in production, which is rare.
4. **QA / test automation with Playwright** — Clubee was literally titled "Low Code Developer and Quality Assurance"; Playwright is on his CV.
5. **Italy** — his own profile says Italy is the one place on-site is a plus. Almost nothing had been aimed there.
6. **Contract marketplaces** — 5 of 7 roles were freelance and he has a Partita IVA, yet zero applications.
7. **Technical Product Manager** — Track D in his own profile, never applied to.
8. **HR / recruitment tech** — he built a CV parser and lives inside these ATSs daily.

## What the sweeps found

**Poland is the highest-yield country for the Power Platform slice.** The European market splits into German/Dutch/Finnish-language partner consultancies (all hard-gated on language) and Polish/Czech B2B contract shops that work in English. All four Power Platform survivors came from the latter.

**Category 3 (coworking/proptech) is effectively dead right now.** OfficeRnD's careers page lists zero roles, Nexudus has none, Cobot/Coworks/Workero/Workbox render nothing, Robin's Lever board is a 404, deskbird and Kadence have no EU engineering openings, Spacebring has only junior Lviv roles. Hostaway is the closest adjacent and every integration role there is Senior or above. **Worth re-checking in a month rather than treating as exhausted.**

## Method notes

- **Ashby is not uniformly a JS shell.** n8n's FDE posting and Vapi's rendered full body copy to WebFetch while their board indexes did not. Worth one fetch before writing an Ashby URL off.
- **Built In is useless as liveness evidence** — every listing checked carried a "this job was removed" stamp.
- Roles that looked alive in search and were dead on the real ATS: Airtable FDE EMEA, Keyrus Portugal, Convera, Workato, ClickHouse, Bitpanda, both Xebia Poland Playwright roles, all three Bubble.io roles.
- **The single most painful language kill:** indigo.ai's Forward Deployed AI Engineer in Milan lists n8n, Make, Zapier, Claude Code and Python — a near-perfect match — but requires Italian *madrelingua*.
