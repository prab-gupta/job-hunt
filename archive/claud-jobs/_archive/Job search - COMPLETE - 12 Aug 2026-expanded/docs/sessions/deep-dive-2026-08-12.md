Plain "AI Engineer" increasingly means prompt engineering and production integration, not ML research — his lane.

**Titles dried up:** junior/mid Solutions Engineer in continental Europe is scarce and gates on German. Nearly every remaining Europe-remote FDE role sits at 5+ years. EMEA Solutions/Sales Engineer roles are London-based and want pre-sales years he does not have.

## The three things costing him applications

1. **No public code.** Required field at Adaptyv, Augment and xAI. One repo with a real n8n workflow or small LLM integration plus a README would unblock a whole category of forms. Third sweep running that this has come up.
2. **No German.** Twelve German-market roles died on language in the previous sweep; more here. B2 German is the highest-leverage fix, because Germany is where the volume of matching roles is.
3. **The salary number.** EUR 30-40k reads junior against a market disclosing 42-120k, and it caps any offer.
