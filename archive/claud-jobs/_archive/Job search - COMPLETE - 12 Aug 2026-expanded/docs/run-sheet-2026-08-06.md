## The branch rule, applied to every form before anything is typed

1. Open the form and look for a cover letter field, by any name: Cover letter, Motivation letter,
   Why do you want to work here, Tell us about yourself, or a letter upload slot.
2. **No such field anywhere** -> fill it in and submit. This is the only case that gets applied to.
3. **The field exists, whether optional or required** -> close it, submit nothing, and record the role
   on the 'Has a cover letter field' tab of the workbook with the field's exact label.

Nothing gets half-filled and abandoned, and no form gets a blank letter box submitted through it.

## Stop and ask rather than guess

An account wall or CAPTCHA. Any payment or government-ID request. A US, UK or Swiss work-authorisation
question. A free-text question asking for his own opinion or a no-AI-generated-response answer. Any
claim about his experience the files do not cover.
