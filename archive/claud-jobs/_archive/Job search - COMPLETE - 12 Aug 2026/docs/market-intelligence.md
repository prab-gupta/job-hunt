## Which titles are being posted

**Growing and worth chasing:**

- **Forward Deployed Engineer** is now a real European category, not US-only. Spyrosoft, Hexa, Arize, Pigment, n8n, gravity9, DeepL, Tangible and A-Players/Poolday all posted one in a single week.
- **GTM Engineer** is the other growth title.
- Plain **"AI Engineer"** increasingly means prompt engineering and production integration, not ML research. Several postings say so explicitly ("this is not a research role", "we don't train models"). That is precisely his lane and it is a recent, favourable shift.
- **AI Automation Engineer** and anything naming n8n/Zapier/Make — far less crowded than generic AI Engineer listings.

**Dried up or gated:**

- Junior and mid **Solutions Engineer** roles in continental Europe are scarce, and the ones that exist gate on German.
- Nearly every remaining "Europe remote" FDE role sits at **5+ years**.
- EMEA Solutions/Sales Engineer roles that do exist are **London-based** and want years of pre-sales he doesn't have.
- **Coworking and proptech is effectively not hiring engineers right now** — OfficeRnD lists zero roles, Nexudus none, Robin's board is a 404, deskbird and Kadence have no EU engineering. Worth re-checking in a month; his OfficeRnD + Nexudus integration experience is genuinely rare.

## Geography

**Poland is the highest-yield country for English-speaking B2B contract work.** The European Power Platform market splits cleanly into German/Dutch/Finnish-language partner consultancies (all hard-gated on language) and Polish/Czech B2B contract shops that work in English. Every Power Platform survivor came from the latter. Same pattern for QA/Playwright contract work.

**Germany has the volume and the language wall.** Twelve German-market roles died on fluent or native German in one sweep alone. hallo theo (2–5 yrs, remote Germany, n8n/Zapier/Airflow/Make) and VOIDS (Hamburg, 3+ yrs, €80–110k) both sat exactly in his band with his exact stack and were unreachable. **B2 German is the single highest-leverage skill investment available to him.**

**Italy is underexploited.** He lives there, would be a local hire, no relocation and no permit conversation. The catch is that Italian-market roles often want Italian: indigo.ai's Forward Deployed AI Engineer in Milan lists n8n, Make, Zapier, Claude Code and Python — a near-perfect match — and requires Italian *madrelingua*. Claranet Italia requires fluency plus Italian residency. Favour English-working-language companies.

**Ireland and Denmark are NOT covered by his EU long-term-residence permit** — both opted out of Directive 2003/109 (confirmed 12 Aug evening; it killed a Greenhouse-company support role and redirects the Supermetrics row to Helsinki over Dublin). Treat Ireland/Denmark like the UK: honest eligibility answer, flag the role.

**The Netherlands has been unusually open** — Avery, Freeday, BAS Group, Lleverage all live, none requiring Dutch.

## Where to search, and where not to

**Renders server-side, verifies cleanly with a fetch:** Greenhouse (both `.io` and `.eu`), Recruitee, Personio, Teamtailor, Lever, join.com, careers subdomains.

**Always a JS shell, needs a browser:** Workable, and *usually* Ashby — but **Ashby is not uniform**: n8n's FDE posting and Vapi's rendered full body copy while their board indexes didn't. Worth one fetch before writing an Ashby URL off.

**Useless as evidence of liveness:**

- **Built In** — every listing checked carried a "this job was removed" stamp
- **RemoteRocketship, Himalayas, Remotive, euremotejobs** — mirrors go stale; three roles "verified" this way were dead on the real ATS
- **Jobgether** — mirrors other companies' posts and is not a real employer page; appeared 7 times in one sweep
- **workingnomads.com** — renders navigation only, confirmed dead end twice

**The 12 Aug "never swept" slice was swept on 12 Aug, late evening.** What it taught:

- **justjoin.it renders fully to a fetcher** (search + offer pages incl. salary bands, deadlines, apply forms) — the workhorse for Poland B2B sweeps. NoFluffJobs and startupjobs.cz are unreachable from the sandboxed fetcher (proxy provenance blocks); bulldogjob is weak; jobs.cz is Czech-language on-site.
- **Getro-hosted VC boards are mostly unsweepable without a JS browser**: only jobs.earlybird.com and jobs.pointnine.com (plus thin hvcapital) are search-indexed; speedinvest, cherry, seedcamp, creandum, balderton, atomico and lakestar returned zero indexed postings. Getro cards also go stale (two caught in one sweep) — always re-verify on the employer ATS. **ycombinator.com/jobs/location/europe is server-rendered and was the best VC-lane discovery page** (workatastartup.com itself 406s, but ycombinator.com job pages fetch cleanly).
- thehub.io renders postings with live Apply buttons; landing.jobs individual /at/ postings fetch fine (its index is robots-blocked). aijobs.net is US-heavy and its search-indexed pages are stale 2024. huggingface.co/jobs is login-gated; mlops.community/jobs 404s; the sifted/tech.eu/eu-startups boards are gone.
- **Deel's Ashby posting API returns an empty array while they are clearly hiring** — a misleading negative; check Deel in a browser only. Retool/monday.com/UiPath board hosts 404 or are proxy-blocked. Textkernel merged into Bullhorn.
- **Category verdict, automation tool vendors: EMPTY in EMEA this month.** Tray.ai (3 roles, all US), Rasa (2 US sales), Notion (FDE is US + 5 yrs), ClickUp (no EU), Gumloop (NA on-site), Relevance AI (no EMEA technical IC). His certifications cash in at partner consultancies (the Avenga / Link Group / Capco pattern), not at the certifying vendors.
- **Category verdict, HR/recruitment tech: thin but non-zero.** HiBob (two roles, bodies unread — browser needed), Remote.com (strong PM row), Greenhouse (Ireland-blocked). Ashby, Oyster, Teamtailor, TestGorilla, Carv, Juicebox, Tellent all confirmed empty for his profile.
- **Category verdict, Technical PM (Track D): genuinely scarce** at ~3 years with EU-only rights — one live verified PM posting (Menhir, Lisbon) plus Remote.com's technical PM. Everything else was US-remote or Senior/Staff/Principal. Track D stays a minor lane.
- Still unswept: Personio, Join, Softr, Landbot, Relay.app, Activepieces, Windmill.dev, Pipedream, Bardeen, Stack AI, Cassidy AI (company-direct); contract-marketplace live check (Malt, Lemon.io, Outvise); coworking/proptech re-check ~early September.

**Attrition is ~50%.** Half of what a sweep returns is dead, gated or hard-blocked when opened in a browser. That is the market, not a failed search.

## Contract marketplaces — LIVE-CHECKED 12 Aug 2026 (evening)

He has a Partita IVA and five of his seven roles were freelance. The live check replaced the earlier training-data guesses; several verdicts changed.

**Do now:**

- **Malt** (malt.it / malt.fr) — open, free signup; the gate is profile completion + validation, no interview. Built on freelancers invoicing through the platform, so Partita IVA is the standard status. Freelancer-side fee 2–10%. Public comps: EUR 130/day (0–2 yrs, Paris) up to **EUR 600/day for a 3–7 yr n8n/AI-agents specialist** (malt.fr, 5.0 rating). **Set EUR 300–350/day, not 250.**
- **freelancermap.com** — open registration, direct client contact per project, no platform commission observed. **The richest public demand found anywhere**: live English-language n8n projects including "REMOTE N8N Workflow Automation Software Engineer Finance London" at **GBP 450–600/day** (posted 12 Aug, ASAP start, 6–12 months), plus N8n Automation Architect / Consultant and Dublin/Cork n8n analyst roles. Roughly half the n8n projects are German-language — filter for the London/Dublin finance cluster.

**Later:** Lemon.io (5-step vetted funnel, ~13 days; their own calculator implies USD 30–40/hr at his level — at/below his anchor), Outvise (contrary to the earlier note there is NO public gig board — demand visible only after signup + certification), Contra (0% commission and USD 40–65/hr automation gigs on the public feed, but thin n8n volume and Italy payout rails unconfirmed — ask support first), Turing (automated vetting; the plentiful LLM-work tier pays USD 15–40/hr).

**Skip:** Worksome (closed to cold signups since 2023, invite-only), Twago (defunct — only a lookalike domain survives), plus the earlier skips (A.Team, Gun.io, Gigster, Toptal, YunoJuno, Comatch, Expert360, Talmix).

**Rate reality check:** his EUR 250–300/day anchor is LOW. Reviewed n8n+AI profiles clear **EUR 400–600/day** in public comps. Entry price EUR 300–350/day, step up after the first one or two platform reviews.
