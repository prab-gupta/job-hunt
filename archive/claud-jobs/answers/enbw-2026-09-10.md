## ATS lesson (new)
careers.enbw.com's file upload (`file_upload` via Chrome tool) reliably freezes the tab's renderer immediately after any successful upload call — reproduced 3 times independently (step-1 resume-parser upload, and step-3 CV upload, across 3 separate fresh tabs). The freeze manifests as `get_page_text`/`find`/`navigate` all timing out with "Page still loading (executeScript waited 45000ms for document_idle)", and eventually a raw CDP `Runtime.evaluate` timeout confirming the renderer itself is unresponsive, not just slow. No amount of waiting (tested up to ~60s cumulative) resolved it; only closing the tab and opening a fresh one worked. Workaround used: never call file_upload on this domain — fill every field manually instead (the site's own guest-apply flow explicitly supports CV-less applications with "we may request the CV at a later stage", which made this a safe, JD-endorsed fallback rather than a compromise). Future agents hitting careers.enbw.com (or other portals sharing this SuccessFactors-esque wizard pattern) should skip file uploads entirely and rely on the structured form fields plus the free-text "why/suitability" questions to carry equivalent CV content.
