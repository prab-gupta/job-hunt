## LEARNINGS (new ATS trap)
Ashby enforces a company-level, not requisition-level, reapplication cooldown (this one: 90 days) tied to the candidate's identity. A candidate who submitted to one Taxfix (or any Ashby-hosted company) requisition will have a *second* application to a *different* requisition at the same company silently rejected at submit time with a "we ask candidates to wait 90 days" banner, even though the dedupe.py/applications.csv same-requisition check passes. Also hit an Ashby form-validation glitch: `browser_fill_form`'s fill() populated the "How do you work with code and technical systems?" textbox visibly, but Ashby's client-side validation still flagged it "Missing entry for required field" on first submit; clicking into the field and typing+deleting one character (to fire a real onChange/keydown event) cleared the false error on the second submit attempt. Future Ashby applications: (1) check applications.csv for ANY prior SUBMITTED row at the same company on the same ATS regardless of requisition, and treat a submission within the last ~90 days as a likely auto-block worth flagging before spending time on the form; (2) after `browser_fill_form`, re-touch each required free-text field with a real keystroke before submitting to avoid stale-validation false negatives.
