## New ATS / process lesson
Nexcess's official careers page (nexcess.net/careers -> redirects to liquidweb.com/careers) embeds
a Jobvite widget (jobs.jobvite.com/liquidweb) that is entirely dead — every path on that Jobvite
site 302s to search.jobvite.com?invalid=1, and the "All open positions" section on the Liquid Web
careers page renders empty. The task brief's own instruction ("if the role is not listed there or
is closed, STATUS: CLOSED") would have produced a false CLOSED verdict here. The real, live ATS
(Rippling) was only found by clicking LinkedIn's actual "Apply" button/link (not by trusting the
"Apply link goes to nexcess.net/careers" note in the queue) and following the resulting new-tab
redirect to ats.rippling.com. Lesson for future rows: when a company's own "careers page" ATS
widget looks broken/empty, re-verify by clicking through LinkedIn's real Apply control before
concluding CLOSED — LinkedIn's off-site apply link is more reliable than a scraped/cached careers
URL.
