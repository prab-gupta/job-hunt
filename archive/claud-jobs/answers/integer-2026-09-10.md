## LEARNINGS
New ATS pattern: some LinkedIn "offsite" postings for Portuguese/nearshore consultancies route through **itjobs.pt**, a Portuguese job-board aggregator that hides the employer's real application email/link behind its own login/registration wall (LinkedIn OAuth or itjobs.pt account), even though the job description itself is fully public. Treat any itjobs.pt-hosted posting the same way as a SuccessFactors-style account wall: NEEDS YOU by default, no account creation.
