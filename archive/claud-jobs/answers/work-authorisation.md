# Work authorisation

Updated 2026-09-07. Use this file over older application notes.

## Facts

Prabhavit is an Indian citizen and a non-EEA national. He holds Italian EU long-term resident status.

- Role performed from Italy: currently authorised to work, sponsorship not needed.
- Role in another participating EU country: not automatically authorised. EU long-term resident status creates a mobility route, but he must apply for and receive that country's residence or work permission. The second country may apply its own labour-market rules.
- Role in Ireland or Denmark: the EU long-term-resident mobility directive does not apply. An ordinary national employment permit or other qualifying immigration permission is needed.
- Role in the UK, Switzerland, US, or another non-covered country: no current work authorisation. Sponsorship or the relevant national permit is needed.
- Remote contractor work performed from Italy through Partita IVA: generally answer that he is authorised where the work is performed, then state that the engagement would be from Italy.

## Standard binary form answers

| Work location | Authorised now? | Need sponsorship or employer-side permit? |
|---|---:|---:|
| Italy, including remote work performed from Italy | Yes | No |
| Another EU country, including Spain, Germany, Sweden, Slovakia, Czechia, Romania, or Portugal | No | Yes |
| Ireland or Denmark | No | Yes |
| UK, Switzerland, US, or another non-covered country | No | Yes |

Do not select "citizen" for any country. Do not select "permanent resident" of the destination country merely because the permit is labelled EU long-term resident. If a form has no accurate option, use "other" or add the transparency line below.

## Transparency line for relocation roles

> I hold Italian EU long-term resident status. I am not currently authorised to work in [country] and would need the applicable employer-side permit or sponsorship process. I am ready to relocate and complete that process.

## Transparency line for remote-from-Italy roles

> I am authorised to work from Italy under Italian EU long-term resident status and can perform the role remotely from Italy. Please confirm whether the role supports an Italian employment or contractor arrangement.

Never call a permit process "routine". Never imply that the Italian permit itself grants immediate work rights in another country.
