## The actual blocker (found only inside the live application form)
Clicked "Apply for this job" on https://careers.redeploy.com/jobs/7489283-genai-developer.
The Teamtailor form opens with a required screening question: "Are you based in Sweden?"
(Yes/No radio, required). Prabhavit is based in Venice, Italy, so the honest answer is No.
Selecting No immediately shows, in the form itself: "You have to meet these requirements to
be able to apply. Even though this role is not a match, we would love to connect with you
for future opportunities, or you can view our other open positions." Every field below that
question (the Stockholm/Göteborg commuting-distance question, how-did-you-hear, why-Redeploy,
the required RAG/agent description, salary, locations, name, email, phone, CV upload, cover
letter, submit) becomes non-interactive: verified via DOM inspection that the underlying
inputs get `pointer-events: none` applied (not just visually greyed, confirmed with
getComputedStyle on the second question's radio inputs). This is a deliberate, ATS-enforced
hard requirement that the candidate already reside in Sweden, not disclosed anywhere in the
LinkedIn posting text or the visible "About the job" JD, only surfaced on the live form.

No workaround was attempted (no forced JS clicks on the pointer-events:none controls, no
false "Yes" answer). Selecting "Yes" would be a fabricated claim (Prabhavit is not based in
Sweden); forcing the disabled controls via script would be circumventing a screening rule the
employer built on purpose. Both are excluded by the standing rules against inventing facts
and against gaming a submission. Correct outcome is to stop and record the kill.
