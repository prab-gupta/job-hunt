## Screening essay answers (submitted verbatim)

Q: Imagine you join a customer project where the product catalogue is highly complex, data quality is poor, several legacy systems need to integrate, and the customer expects a solution within a tight deadline. What would you do in your first few weeks?
A: This is close to a problem I have already lived through. At FlowSpace, I took over a live marketplace built on a legacy stack and had to migrate its data onto a new Next.js and Laravel platform while the product stayed live and grew from 2,000 to 4,500 plus users. In the first few weeks I would do the same three things I did there. First, map the existing data model entity by entity so I understand exactly where the quality problems and inconsistencies sit before proposing a fix. Second, decide the integration path per entity rather than one blanket approach, in my case that meant choosing between direct API endpoints or CSV exports depending on what the legacy system could reliably expose. Third, agree with the customer on a narrow, testable first slice that can go live and be checked against real data quickly, rather than trying to model the whole catalogue perfectly before writing any integration code. I would also build lightweight validation tooling early, since data quality problems only become visible once you can query and compare the migrated result against the source.

Q: How do you balance hands-on coding with being a technical advisor to customers and other engineers?
A: I default to writing the first version of anything myself so I understand the real constraints, then step back into an advisory role once the pattern is proven. At FlowSpace I built the AI listing bot and the support AI agents myself end to end, and once the pattern for human in the loop review and confidence based escalation was working, my job became explaining that pattern to the rest of the team so they could extend it. I try to stay hands-on enough that I am never advising from theory, and I make time to review other people's designs and unblock them, which is the same balance this role is asking for. I would rather ship a rough working version and refine it against real customer data than spend the same time only advising on a design that has not been tested.
