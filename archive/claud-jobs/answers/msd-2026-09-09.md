## LEARNINGS
MSD's Workday tenant (msd.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com) has no guest/apply-without-account path, matching the pattern already seen on Zendesk's and Amadeus's tenants: "Apply Manually" still requires creating an account (email + password) before step 1. Also: clicking a LinkedIn "Apply on company website" link via a synthetic ref-click did not register as a trusted user gesture (no new tab opened); a coordinate-based `computer left_click` on the same element did trigger the `target="_blank"` navigation. Prefer coordinate clicks over ref clicks for links expected to open a new tab.
