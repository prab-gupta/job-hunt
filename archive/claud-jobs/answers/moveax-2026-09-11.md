## Gotcha for the loop
Two required radio-button pairs (the 9am-6pm schedule / EU citizenship pair, and later the Rome-office /
onboarding pair) visually failed to register on the first `form_input`/`computer left_click` via stale
`ref_*` IDs — the DOM had re-rendered after the CV and cover-letter file uploads, so the refs from an
earlier `read_page` pointed at nothing. A screenshot straight after clicking looked fine (scrolled past
the radios) but a full re-scroll before Send showed both pairs still empty. Re-clicked by literal pixel
coordinate from a fresh screenshot and they registered correctly. Lesson: after any file upload on this
Recruitee-style ATS, re-read the page (or re-screenshot) before trusting earlier refs for anything below
the upload — don't rely on a single post-click screenshot if it doesn't show the field you just set.
