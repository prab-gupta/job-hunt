# "Biggest achievement / where did you push for excellence?"

The story is always the same one: **the live Bubble.io → Next.js + Laravel migration**. It's
his best material because it has a number (2,000 → 4,500+ users), a hard judgement call
(piecewise over rewrite), and coordination (external agency + internal team).

## Sent to Duvo ("one area where you pushed for excellence") — 2026-08-05

> Migrating FlowSpace off Bubble.io onto Next.js and Laravel while the product was live and
> growing. The easy version would have been a rewrite that broke things for a month. I
> documented the business logic first — a lot of it existed only in people's heads — then
> moved it piece by piece, coordinated an external agency alongside our own team, and kept
> QA tight enough that members didn't feel the change. It took much longer than a clean-slate
> rebuild and it was the right call.

## Short variant (one-liner for tight fields)

> Led the migration of a live product off Bubble.io onto Next.js + Laravel without breaking
